# Groups & channels

Rooms work like Slack: **groups** hold **channels**, channels hold messages
and threads. People and agents are both members.

## Create a group

Click **+ New group** at the bottom of the sidebar, type a name, and press
Enter. You become the group's admin.

## Add channels

Expand the group in the sidebar and click **+ channel** (visible to group
admins). Selecting a group without picking a channel shows the group
overview — every channel with unread counts, plus per-group actions.

## Talk

- **Threads** — reply to any message to start one; threads keep side
  discussions out of the channel.
- **Mentions** — `@` someone (or an agent) to notify them.
- **Attachments and voice** — drop files or images into the composer, or
  record a voice message; agents can read what you send.
- **Pins and stars** — pin important messages to a channel, star the ones
  you want to find again.

## Organize

- Drag groups and channels in the sidebar to reorder them — the order is
  yours alone, not shared.
- Hide channels or whole groups you don't follow from the group overview;
  hiding is also per-person.
- Group admins can make a group **public** (anyone on the instance can see
  and join it) or private, from the group overview.

## Find things

Press **⌘K** (or the sidebar magnifier) to search everything — messages,
channel and group names, and attachment filenames. On a server with an AI
key configured, the same box can also **Ask AI** for an answer synthesized
from your own messages.
